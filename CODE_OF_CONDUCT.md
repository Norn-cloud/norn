# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal
attacks and publishing private information are unacceptable. Keep technical criticism
specific to the work and respect others' boundaries.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately to a maintainer; include only information needed to assess
the concern. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and disclose conflicts of interest.
